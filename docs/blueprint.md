# **App Name**: GiftArtStudio

## Core Features:

- Personalized Gift Gallery: An interactive, searchable grid for browsing products like backpacks, lunch bags, and toys with high-quality visual cards.
- Product Customizer Tool: A dynamic interface that allows users to enter names and see a real-time preview of their custom gift design.
- AI Personalization Assistant: An AI-powered message generation tool that suggests unique gift notes and greeting card messages based on the recipient's interests.
- Integrated Reviews & Testimonials: A community-driven section displaying customer photos, ratings, and social proof directly from happy parents.
- Centralized Product Inventory: A database to manage gift catalog data, categories, and inventory status efficiently.
- Shopping Cart & Checkout: A streamlined shopping experience allowing users to collect personalized items and process payments via various methods.
- Social Media Hub: A feed displaying the studio's latest creations from Instagram to inspire gift-giving ideas.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Soft Blush Pink (#FF90B1), chosen for its warm, childlike, and joyful associations.
- Background Color: Shell Pink Whisper (#FFF9FA), a highly desaturated variant of the primary for a soft, breathable workspace.
- Accent Color: Orchid Magenta (#CE4EBA), an analogous hue used for calls-to-action to provide strong contrast.
- Headline Font: 'Poppins' (Sans-Serif), utilized for its friendly geometric shapes; Body Font: 'PT Sans' (Sans-Serif) for modern readability and warmth.
- A modular, grid-centric layout with soft-rounded corners and significant whitespace to emphasize playful imagery.
- Subtle cloud-like floating animations and soft hover transitions on product cards for a whimsical feel.
- Hand-drawn, thin-stroke icons that evoke the creative and handcrafted nature of custom studio gifts.
